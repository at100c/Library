这是指引，不是法规Guidance — not regulation

本书为本拿比市府提供一套框架与要求，用于创建高质量的持牌托儿设施。The Child Care Design Principles and Guidelines were created to provide the City of Burnaby with a framework and set of requirements for creating high-quality licensed child care facilities.p8

这些原则与指引是对现行法规、立法与标准的补充：它们不取代省或本拿比市的任何监管要求，也不保证托儿设施能获发牌照；满足现行监管要求仍是开发方的责任。The principles and guidelines complement existing regulations, legislation, and standards. They do not replace any provincial or City of Burnaby regulatory requirements, and they do not guarantee that the child care facility will be licensed.p8

原书建议完整审阅全部 7 项原则及其指引——它们被设计为一个整体框架。For best results, a comprehensive review of all seven principles and their corresponding guidelines is recommended. They are intended to function as a cohesive framework.p9

先猜再看Guess first

猜一猜：本拿比要求每名儿童至少多少室内可用面积？Guess: what is the minimum usable indoor floor space per child?

答案：3.7 m²。BC 许可法规规定每名儿童最低 3.7 m² 可用室内面积，本书原则重申同一数字；且只计儿童活动空间——储藏、洗手间、员工区、厨房、门厅、通道、结构柱等一律不算。Answer: 3.7 m². The Regulations mandate a minimum of 3.7 m² of usable indoor floor area per child, counted only in activity spaces used by the children.p15

七大原则总览The seven principles at a glance

  • 1 · 选址、场地与安全到达Location, siting and safe access — 中心必须选在安全、无障碍的地段，以营造社区感并建立家长信任。Centres must be in safe, accessible areas that foster a sense of community and build trust among families.p11
  • 2 · 儿童 — 室内空间Children — indoor spaces — 每名儿童至少 3.7 m² 可用室内面积，并与室外游乐场安全直接相连。A minimum of 3.7 m² of usable indoor floor space per child, with a safe, direct connection to the outdoor playground.p11
  • 3 · 儿童 — 室外游乐场Children — outdoor playground — 每个项目有专用游乐场（含遮盖与露天区），每名儿童至少 6 m² 可用面积。Dedicated outdoor playgrounds for each program, with a minimum of 6 m² of usable space per child.p11
  • 4 · 家庭Families — 中心必须设计成亲切、包容的空间，促进家庭参与。Centres must be designed to be welcoming, inclusive spaces that promote family involvement.p11
  • 5 · 员工 — 工作环境Staff — workplace environment — 中心必须包含员工支持空间，营造积极、健康、高效的工作环境。Centres must include support spaces for staff to promote a positive, healthy, and productive work environment.p11
  • 6 · 无障碍、包容与公平Accessibility, inclusion and equity — 中心必须完全包容且无障碍，让不同能力与背景的儿童都能有意义地参与。Centres must be designed to be fully inclusive and accessible for children of diverse abilities, developmental needs and backgrounds.p11
  • 7 · 韧性与未来适应Resiliency and futureproofing — 中心必须灵活、耐用、运营成本高效，并融入抵御气候变化的策略。Centres must be designed to be flexible, durable, cost efficient to operate and incorporate climate change-resistant strategies.p11

为什么写这本书：本拿比的托儿布局Why this guide: Burnaby's child care context

  • 市议会在 1990 年代初通过首份本拿比儿童保育政策，目标是为居民提供多元托儿选择，这份承诺延续至今。This commitment goes back to the early 1990s with Council's adoption of the first Burnaby Child Care Policy, aimed at providing residents with diverse child care options.p7
  • 市府已建成 7 座市属托儿设施，共 331 个名额。Securing 7 City-owned child care facilities (with 331 spaces).p7
  • 市府与本拿比校区正式合作，设立最多 12 座托儿设施。Formal collaboration with the Burnaby School District for the establishment of up to 12 child care facilities.p7
  • 2021 年 7 月，市议会通过新的《本拿比儿童保育行动计划》——一份 10 年路线图，聚焦增加供给、强化伙伴关系、改善可负担性与提升质量。In July 2021, Burnaby Council approved the City's new Burnaby Child Care Action Plan, a 10-year roadmap focused on increasing access, strengthening partnerships, improving affordability and enhancing quality.p8
  • 行动计划承诺：到 2030 年新增 4,400 个托儿名额。Creating 4,400 new child care spaces by 2030.p8
  • 2022 年 5 月，市议会同意确立一套原则、价值观、准则与指引，协助市府开发优质托儿空间——本书即由此而来。In May of 2022, Council agreed to confirm a set of principles, values, criteria, and guidelines to assist the City in the development of exceptional child care spaces.p8
「一个从婴儿期到小学低年级都在托儿项目里的孩子，在托儿所度过的时间会超过全部在校时间；在幼儿期，他清醒的时间在托儿所可能比在家里还多。」"A child who attends a child care program from infancy through young school-age years will spend more time in child care than all the hours of schooling, and in their early years may spend more waking hours in child care than at home." — Jim Greenman, Caring Spaces, Learning Places (2005)p6

托儿空间必须做到的 9 件事（§1.0）What child care spaces must do (§1.0)

原书开篇说，这些空间必须：让儿童能大小组相处；有独处空间；有安全舒适、不受打扰的休息睡眠处；能自由移动、安全探索；随儿童需求与能力变化而灵活调整；室内外直接连通、来回贯通；温暖亲切、亲近自然、有启发性与挑战性；提供正面的饮食体验；包容并带来归属感。These spaces must: allow children to be with other children in large and small groups; offer spaces for children to be on their own; have safe, comfortable places to rest or sleep undisturbed; enable children to move freely and explore safely; be flexible and adapt as needs change; flow directly from indoors to outdoors and back; be warm, inviting, inspired by nature, stimulating and challenging; offer a positive experience for eating and drinking; and be inclusive with a sense of belonging.p7

七大原则逐条（§2.0）The seven principles, one by one (§2.0)

本拿比市确立了 7 项关键设计原则，目标是让托儿中心成为滋养心智、激发学习兴趣的空间。The City of Burnaby has committed to seven key design principles to ensure child care centres are spaces that nurture young minds and spark a love of learning.p11

1 · 选址、场地与安全到达Location, siting and safe access

「托儿中心必须选在安全、无障碍的地段，精心选址，以营造社区感并建立家庭信任。」→ 本站第 3 页 §3.1"Child care centres must be in safe, accessible areas that are carefully chosen to foster a sense of community and build trust among families." → Page 3 (§3.1)p11

§3.1 选址与安全到达§3.1 Location, Siting and Safe Access

原则 1：中心必须选在安全、无障碍的地段，营造社区感、建立家庭信任。Section 3 的原则与指引在详细设计与开发阶段必须遵守。Principle 1: centres must be in safe, accessible areas that foster community and trust. Section 3 must be followed during detailed design and development.p13

托儿中心必须选址在……（must be located）Child care centres must be located…

  • 与学校、社区中心、公园等家庭常用服务同处或邻近With or near other services used by families like schools, community centres, and parksp13
  • 预计有较多家庭居住或工作的新住宅中心或商业区In new residential centres or commercial areas where larger numbers of families can be expected to live or workp13
  • 靠近公共交通线路Close to public transit routesp13
  • 步行易达、与周边社区连通良好的区域In areas with easy pedestrian access and good connectivity to the local neighbourhoodp13
  • 远离繁忙交通走廊、商业装卸区、工业用途，以及一切嘈杂、有害、危险的环境Away from busy traffic corridors, commercial loading zones, industrial uses and from all noisy, noxious, and dangerous environmentsp13
  • 建筑从室外到室内无障碍通行（如需台阶须设坡道）With barrier-free access from the exterior to the interior of the building (i.e. providing a ramp if stairs are required)p13
  • 专用室外游乐空间与室内空间无缝连接、直接相邻Where dedicated outdoor playground space is seamlessly connected and directly adjacent to the indoor spacesp13
  • 有充足自然光的地方Where ample natural light is availablep13
  • 能为不同年龄儿童提供服务连续体的空间（见术语表）In spaces which can offer a continuum of services for children of various agesp13

托儿中心必须具备……（must have）Child care centres must have…

  • 良好的导向标识Good wayfinding signagep14
  • 照明良好的入口，附近有安全的接送停车Well-lit entry points with safe close by access for parking for pick up and drop offp14
  • 以对讲系统管控的安全入口；外门与大门装本地报警器Secure building entry access using intercoms and local alarms on exterior doors and gatesp14
  • 摄像头只许装在建筑周界与室外出入口——不许装在儿童活动区域Security cameras for perimeter and exterior entrances/exits only (i.e. not in areas occupied by children)p14

§3.2 儿童 — 室内空间§3.2 Children — Indoor Spaces

原则 2：每名儿童至少 3.7 m² 可用室内面积。儿童学习环境必须放松、居家、舒适、亲切。Principle 2: at least 3.7 m² of usable indoor space per child. Learning environments must be relaxed, home-like, comfortable and welcoming.p13

室内空间必须开敞、灵活，采用天然饰面、温暖柔和的色彩与充足自然光；并与室外游乐场有安全、直接的视觉与实体连接。Interior spaces must be wide open, flexible, with natural finishes, warm muted colours, and ample natural light — plus a safe and direct connection, both visually and physically, to the outdoor playground.p15p15

多项目可共享的空间（有条件）Spaces multiple programs may share (with conditions)

多项目中心可考虑共享空间——但可能需要额外面积，且平面布局须能让各项目安全、方便地到达这些关键空间，否则共享不可行。Centres with multiple programs may consider sharing spaces — however, additional square footage may be required, and sharing may not be possible if the floor plan does not allow easy, safe access.p15

可共享候选：厨房、洗衣房、成人无障碍洗手间、员工办公室、员工休息室、中心内的垃圾/回收/堆肥区、项目与家长婴儿车储藏。Shareable candidates: kitchens, laundry rooms, adult fully accessible washrooms, staff office, staff break room, garbage/recycling/compost areas within the centre, program and parent stroller storage.p15

共享厨房的例外：服务 18 个月以上儿童的每个项目仍须有自己的冰箱与微波炉。Kitchen-sharing exception: each program serving children over 18 months old needs its own refrigerator and microwave.p15

主活动室解剖Anatomy of the main activity room

机构与空间术语Facility and space terms

托儿设施 / 托儿中心Child care facility / centre

容纳一个或多个项目的建筑或建筑的一部分。A building or portion of a building which houses one or more programs.p33

项目（program）Child care program

拥有自己专用房间的一组儿童。A group of children that have their own room or rooms.p33

主活动室Main activity room

室内最大的空间，其他空间通常围绕它连接布置。The largest of the inside spaces, with the other spaces typically connected and arranged around it.p33

大运动 / 午睡室Gross motor / nap room

大型多功能空间：兼作儿童睡眠室（通常每天 2–3 小时），其余时间作大活动空间。A large multi-purpose space that functions as both a sleep room for children (typically 2–3 hours per day) and a large activity space during the rest of the day.p33

婴儿床 / 午睡室Crib / nap room

独立的单一用途睡眠室，为 18 个月以下儿童设婴儿床位。A separate dedicated single purpose sleep room with spaces for cribs (for children under 18 months old).p33

安静室Quiet room

独立房间，供小群体进行较安静的活动，也是儿童暂时离开大群体的去处。A separate room for a variety of quieter activities involving small groups of children and a place for children to be away from the large group.p33

台面与服务术语Counter and service terms

员工沟通与工作区Staff communication and workspace

全高台面，供员工记录关键信息、查阅档案与接打电话。A full height counter for staff to log critical information, access records and take phone calls.p33

家庭台Family counter

全高台面，家庭在此每日签到签退孩子，并获取育儿信息与项目资源。A full height counter where families sign their children in and out of child care daily and where parenting information and program resources are provided.p33

多功能 / 过渡迎宾空间Multi-purpose / transitional welcome space

进入项目前的舒适空间：让家庭安静地帮孩子过渡，也可休息、与人连接；营造到达感，并可作文化展示焦点。A comfortable space, prior to entering the program, which offers families a quiet time to ease the transition for their child, a space for resting and connecting with others, a sense of arrival, and a focal point for cultural representation.p33

服务连续体Continuum of services

一处设施内设多个项目、服务出生至 5 岁儿童；核心理念是孩子/家庭可留在同一地点，直到孩子上学。More than one program in a child care facility, serving children birth to 5 years — children/families can stay in one location until the child leaves for school.p33
