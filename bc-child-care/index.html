Guidance — not regulation这是指引，不是法规

Developed by the Province of British Columbia, the guidelines provide consistent guidance above and beyond regulatory requirements, aiming for higher, more consistent quality centres that are accessible and inclusive for all.本书由 BC 省制定，为全省新建托儿中心的设计提供一致指引，内容高于法规要求，目标是让全省托儿中心更优质、更一致，且对所有人无障碍、包容。p8

They do not repeat or replace regulatory requirements, nor do they guarantee that building permit or licensing applications will meet regulatory requirements.它不重复、不取代法规要求，也不保证建筑许可或托儿牌照申请能通过审批。p7

3.7 m²/孩

Licensing floor for usable floor area per child in the Activity Area (Child Care Licensing Regulation). Excluded from the calculation: counters and millwork, staff work space beside counters, door swings and access space, and internal pathways where activity centres cannot be set up.室内活动区可用面积牌照底线（《Child Care Licensing Regulation》）。计算时扣除：台面与木作占地、台面旁员工工作区、门扇开启与通往相邻空间所需面积、无法布置活动区的内部通道。p60

14 m²/孩

Outdoor Play Area per child — 14 m² for every program type (Table 30).室外活动区（Outdoor Play Area）人均面积——五类项目一律 14 m²/孩（Table 30）。p83

30%

Grossing factor: total area adds 30% over net area for walls, corridors and circulation, mechanical and electrical rooms (Appendix 7.2).毛面积系数 grossing factor：总面积 = 净面积之上再加 30%，涵盖墙体、走廊与流通、机电房等（附录 7.2 口径）。p88

±20% → ±5%

Four classes of cost estimate: Class D ±20%, Class C ±15%, Class B ±10%, Class A ±5% — accuracy improves as design progresses.造价估算四级精度：Class D ±20% → Class C ±15% → Class B ±10% → Class A ±5%，随设计阶段推进而收窄。p25

§1 Background and Design Principles§1 背景与设计原则

Section 1.0 Background and Context (§1.1–§1.4)Section 1.0 Background and Context（§1.1–§1.4）

Intent and scope文件意图与适用范围

The guidelines serve child care centres offering licensed group child care for children aged birth to 12 years, delivered through ground-up builds, modular builds, major renovations and/or additions.本书适用于提供持牌集体托儿（licensed group child care）、服务出生至 12 岁儿童的托儿中心，覆盖新建、模块化建造、重大翻新与扩建项目。p8

  • Written for full-time centre-based care — not for child care in personal residences or occasional or recreational care.面向全职、中心式照护；不是为家庭住所内的托儿项目或其他类型（如临时、娱乐型照护）编写。p11
  • The guidelines do not cover developing or operating a child care program; a thorough business plan should be considered alongside the design phases.本书不提供托儿项目的开发与运营指引；设计阶段应同时考虑一份周全的商业计划。p8
  • Design and construction professionals are expected to rely on their professional judgment in applying these guidelines.设计与施工专业人员应运用专业判断来落实这些指引。

The five design principles (§1.4, in full)五大设计原则（§1.4 逐条）

1 · Culturally rich, welcoming, responsive文化丰富、亲切、呼应社区

Child care centres are culturally rich, welcoming and responsive to the communities in which they are situated. They foster a strong sense of belonging and cultural safety for children, families, caregivers and early childhood professionals using the space.托儿中心文化丰富、亲切待人，呼应所在社区；为使用空间的儿童、家庭、照护者与幼教专业人员营造强烈的归属感与文化安全感。p12

2 · Living and learning together, inclusively在包容中共同生活与学习

Child care centres facilitate children's respectful living and learning together through an inclusive and accessible design that enables relationships, play and learning.托儿中心以包容、无障碍的设计，让儿童在关系、游戏与学习中互相尊重地共同生活与学习。p12

3 · Enabling early childhood professionals支持幼教专业人员

Child care centres enable early childhood professionals to deliver quality, inclusive care and learning environments and facilitate healthy workplaces that support early childhood professional recruitment and retention.托儿中心让幼教专业人员能够提供优质、包容的照护与学习环境，并营造健康的工作场所，支持幼教专业人员的招聘与留任。p12

4 · Functional, durable, climate-resilient耐用、高效、气候韧性

Child care centres are functional, durable and long-lasting, cost- and energy-efficient to run, incorporate climate-resilient strategies and consider environmental impacts over the lifetime of the centre.托儿中心功能完善、坚固耐用、历久长效，运营成本与能耗高效，融入气候韧性策略，并考虑中心全生命周期内的环境影响。p12

5 · Safe, accessible, health-promoting安全、无障碍、促进健康

Child care centres are safe and accessible and promote the health and well-being of all children, early childhood professionals and families.托儿中心安全且无障碍，促进所有儿童、幼教专业人员与家庭的健康与福祉。p12

Human rights and inclusion人权与包容

  • B.C.'s Human Rights Code helps make sure everyone can participate equally in daily life; centres must comply with all applicable laws, including the Code.《BC 人权法典》（B.C. Human Rights Code）保障人人平等参与日常生活；托儿中心必须遵守包括该法典在内的所有适用法律。p12
  • Consistent with the Province's commitment to reconciliation, the guidelines support design consistent with the rights of Indigenous Peoples in the UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples.与省府的和解承诺一致：本书的建议、考量与资源旨在支持与《联合国原住民权利宣言》所表达的原住民集体与个人人权相符的设计路径。p12
  • Inclusion is a fundamental human right: it happens when the unique differences that make up individuals — race, ethnicity, culture, gender, sexuality, family structure, values, socioeconomic status, physical and cognitive ability, learning needs and health care needs — are valued and celebrated.包容是基本人权：当构成每个人的独特差异——种族、民族、文化、性别、性取向、家庭结构、价值观、社会经济地位、身心能力、学习需求与健康照护需求——被珍视与庆祝时，包容就发生了。p13
  • Design with flexibility and accessibility in mind — spaces easy for people using wheelchairs or other mobility devices are easier for everyone.以灵活性与无障碍为出发点设计，以适应个人与群体不断变化的需求——例如让轮椅等行动辅助设备使用者能轻松移动的空间，对所有人都更方便。p13
  • Culturally appropriate design goes beyond cosmetic features — e.g., environments that allow cultural practices such as cultural cooking.文化适宜的设计要超越表面装饰，考虑由文化因素塑造的独特空间使用方式，例如为反映社区的文化烹饪实践设计环境。p13
  • Use universal images in signage to support wayfinding regardless of spoken language, and written languages reflecting the community — e.g., First Nations languages, Michif, Braille.标识使用通用图像（如洗手间标志）以跨越口语差异支持寻路；标识文字应反映所服务社区的语言，如原住民语言、Michif、盲文等。p13

Early planning早期规划（Early Planning）

The concept is developed here — vision, program philosophies and goals — informed by the §1.4 design principles. Typical activities:这一阶段形成托儿中心的概念：确立愿景、项目理念与具体目标，并以 §1.4 五大设计原则为参照。典型活动包括：p19

  • Considering community need; engaging the local child care community; engaging First Nations, Métis and Inuit communities; determining program types and numbers; understanding local government requirements, building Acts and bylaws, and the licensing regulation with the Regional Health Authority licensing office.考虑社区需求；与本地托儿社群互动；与原住民、梅蒂人与因纽特人社群互动；确定要创建的项目类型与名额；理解地方政府要求与现行规划；理解相关建筑法规；理解牌照法规并咨询地区卫生局牌照办公室。p19
  • Children spend a large part of their day in the centre; the environment, sometimes called the "third teacher," shapes their experiences.儿童在中心度过一天中的大部分时间——环境有时被称为「第三位老师」（the third teacher），塑造着儿童的体验。p20
  • Trauma-informed care can inform planning and design — minimizing known stressors, particularly those suggesting control and concealed observation such as one-way glazing or institutional-looking elements.创伤知情照护（trauma-informed care）可作为早期规划与设计的参考：它认识创伤的广泛影响，营造尽量减少再创伤的环境，例如减少已知压力源，特别是单向玻璃、机构化外观等暗示控制与隐蔽观察的元素。p20
  • Understand community demand and service gaps (sources include Statistics Canada Census Profile, B.C. Child Care Map, CCRR programs and the Licensing Officer); then prepare a thorough business plan for operational and financial viability.确定项目类型与数量时，要摸清社区需求与现有服务的缺口；信息来源包括加拿大统计局人口普查、B.C. Child Care Map、本地 Child Care Resource and Referral 项目与卫生局牌照官。确定类型与数量后，应做一份周全的商业计划以确保运营与财务可行。p22
  • For maximum group sizes and child-to-staff ratios, consult Schedule E of the Child Care Licensing Regulation.最大组规模与师生比：查阅牌照法规附表 E（Schedule E）。p22

Site evaluation and project feasibility (§2.4)场地评估与项目可行性（§2.4）

Careful site evaluation helps avoid projects failing on expense, difficulty, impracticality or inability to license. A feasibility study — usually led by the design team — weighs location and access, zoning, health and environmental factors. The site must fit the building, outdoor play area and exterior support spaces, with indoors and outdoors adjacent and at the same grade where possible.认真评估拟选场地，可避免项目因费用、难度、不切实际或无法取得牌照而中途夭折。可行性研究通常由设计团队主导，评估位置与出入、分区与开发要求、健康与福祉、环境因素。场地大小必须容纳建筑、室外游乐区与室外支持空间；室内外应尽量相邻且同标高。p23

§4.5 School Age Care§4.5 学龄儿童照顾 School Age Care

  • The guidance throughout the book applies to school age programs, ideally located close to or on school grounds, or with a safe, viable transition plan.本书全部设计指引同样适用于学龄项目；这类项目理想上靠近学校或位于校地上，否则运营方要有安全可行的接送方案。
  • School age care often runs in shared school or community spaces (classrooms, gyms, multi-purpose rooms, libraries); designing for dual use is not addressed in this document.学龄照护常在学校或社区的共用空间（教室、体育馆、多功能室、图书馆）里办——共用空间的双重用途如何设计，本书不展开。
  • Sufficient, accessible storage — especially in shared spaces — for timely set-up and take-down, with lockable storage where appropriate; personal storage sized for children's backpacks and supplies.要有充足易达的储物（共用空间尤其如此），支持项目快速布置与收纳，合适时含可锁储物；儿童背包与用品要有尺寸合适的个人储物。
  • Space for varied activities across developmental stages — social and interactive games, imaginative play, art, reading and writing — with cozy reading or lounge areas and a portion identified for older children.空间要支持多种活动与不同发展阶段，促进灵活性、选择与（合适时的）自主性：社交互动游戏、想象游戏、艺术项目、阅读写作等；阅读/休息区配大而舒适的家具，营造温馨归属感；可划出一块标识为大龄儿童的区域。

Two kinds of exterior space两类室外区域

A high-quality Outdoor Play Area matters as much as indoor space. Exterior space has two parts: the Outdoor Play Area (the play zones a centre chooses to incorporate) and Exterior Support Spaces (Outdoor Storage, Program Stroller Storage, Family/Caregiver Stroller Storage and the Outdoor Garbage and Recycling Area).高质量的室外游乐区对儿童健康发展的重要性不亚于室内空间。室外区域分两类：Outdoor Play Area（室外游乐区）——中心按可用空间、位置、项目类型等选择纳入的各类室外游戏分区；Exterior Support Spaces（室外支持空间）——支持中心运营、维护与功能活动的室外空间，包括室外储物、项目婴儿车存放、家庭/照护者婴儿车存放与室外垃圾回收区。p64

Large, fixed play equipment is not mandatory in B.C. It is costly to replace, can become outdated as safety requirements evolve, and its fall zones reduce free-play area. Alternatives mirror the natural environment with materials children can manipulate — sand, dirt, gravel and water.大型固定游乐设备在 BC 并非强制（见 Director of Licensing Standards of Practice – Safe Play Space）；它更换成本高、安全要求演变会使其过时，且所需的跌落区会减少儿童自由游戏的面积。原书建议考虑替代方案：让户外环境贴近自然环境，用沙、土、砾石、水等可让儿童动手塑造的材料。p65

Layout布局

  • The Outdoor Play Area is directly adjacent to the Indoor Activity Area, with physical and visual connection.室外游乐区与室内活动区直接相邻，物理与视觉相连，成人易于在室内外之间移动与沟通。
  • The Outdoor Play Area is at the same grade as the Indoor Activity Area for easy, immediate access.室外游乐区与室内活动区同标高，可即时、方便地到达户外。
  • A variety of play zones for active play, rest and quiet time, visually and functionally cohesive.纳入多种游戏分区，支持儿童的活跃游戏、休息与安静时间需求；各分区在视觉与功能上衔接成统一的游乐区。
  • Support spaces arranged for efficient functioning; the play area may sit near a Children's Washroom; noisier zones away from quieter ones, with sightlines kept in view.支持空间的安排要支持中心高效运转并最大化游乐空间的使用与乐趣；游乐区可近儿童洗手间；吵闹活跃区与安静区可分开，并留意视线。
  • Wherever possible, locate the play area away from parking lots and roadways; consider an outdoor handwashing station.游乐区尽量远离停车场与道路，减少污染物接触并保证安全；可考虑户外洗手站。

7.1 Glossary of Terms and Definitions (26 terms)7.1 术语表（26 条）

Term术语Definition定义
Accessibility
无障碍		Accessibility means that all people can take part in their communities through work, play and other daily activities. It is about removing barriers and increasing inclusion and independence for everyone.所有人都能通过工作、游戏与日常活动参与社区；无障碍关乎消除障碍、增加每个人的包容与独立性（定义引自《Accessible British Columbia Act》通俗摘要）。
Barrier
障碍		Anything that hinders the full and equal participation in society of a person with an impairment — caused by environments, attitudes, practices, policies, information, communications or technologies.任何妨碍残障人士充分平等参与社会的事物，可由环境、态度、做法、政策、信息、传播或技术造成，并受交叉形式歧视影响（定义出自《Accessible British Columbia Act》）。
Building envelope
建筑围护结构		The physical barrier separating the inside of a building from the outside.分隔建筑内部与外部的实体屏障。
Child care needs assessment
托儿需求评估		An assessment of the community's child care needs, often including population and growth statistics, available spaces, and strategies, actions and partnerships.对社区托儿需求的评估，常含社区儿童人口与增长统计、现有托儿名额，并提出支持与应对需求的策略、行动与伙伴关系。
Co-located
共置		Two or more child care programs placed in the same building.两个或以上托儿项目位于同一栋建筑内。
Cultural safety
文化安全		Culturally safe child care meets the distinct needs and interests of First Nations, Métis, Inuit people and Indigenous organizations with equitable access to affordable, inclusive, quality, culturally relevant programs and services.文化安全的托儿服务满足原住民、梅蒂人、因纽特人与原住民组织的独特需求与利益，并让他们公平获得可负担、包容、优质且文化相关的项目与服务。
Design team
设计团队		The team of design professionals, usually led by an architect, responsible for the design of the centre including the site plan, interior and exterior spaces.负责托儿中心设计的设计专业人员团队，通常由建筑师领导，涵盖场地规划、室内与室外空间；具体专业构成视项目而定。
Fall zone
跌落区		The area around playground equipment designed to protect children from serious injuries if they fall off the equipment.游乐设备周围、为在儿童跌落时防止严重伤害而设计的区域。
Feasibility study
可行性研究		An assessment and analysis of the suitability of the proposed project for child care — bylaw reviews, geotechnical reviews, archeological studies, legal and topographical surveys.对拟建项目是否适合托儿的评估与分析，可含附例审查、岩土审查、考古研究、法律与地形测量。
Functional relationship diagram
功能关系图		A schematic graphic identifying the rooms and spaces and the key functional connections between them. They are not floor plans and should be read with the associated narrative.标示托儿中心各房间与空间及其关键功能联系的示意图形。本书各图不是平面图，应结合相应文字阅读。
Grossing factor
毛面积系数		An estimate of the additional space over and above the listed room areas needed to develop a functional child care centre — wall thicknesses, internal corridors and circulation, mechanical and electrical rooms.在所列房间面积之上、为建成可用的托儿中心所需的额外面积估算，包括墙厚、内部走廊与流通、机电房。
Hardscape
硬质景观		The built features used in landscape architecture, such as paths or walls.景观建筑中使用的建成要素，如路径或墙体。
Inclusion
包容		Supporting all people to participate fully within society regardless of abilities, identities or backgrounds, with equitable access and the supports needed to fully participate and belong.支持所有人不论能力、身份或背景都能充分参与社会；人人都有公平的机会与所需的支持来充分参与并获得归属感。
Meaningful participation
有意义的参与		A child's role is valued by all involved including the child; more than being present — actively engaged, with involvement beyond an appearance of equity.儿童的角色被包括儿童本人在内的所有参与者珍视；不只是身在环境之中，而是积极投入，且其参与不只是表面上的公平。
Net area
净面积		Area of a programmed space, typically measured from inside wall surface to inside wall surface.已编入计划的空间面积，通常由内墙面量至内墙面。
People with diverse abilities
多元能力人士		The term "diverse abilities" may be used in place of "disabilities" based on the personal preference of a child and their family; it positively focuses on all people being different, but able.「多元能力」可按儿童及其家庭的个人偏好替代「残障」一词；它包容所有人，积极地看待每个人的不同与能力。
People with support needs
有支持需求人士		People experiencing, or at risk of, developmental delay or disability who require support to optimize development, functional abilities and quality of life; not limited to people with a diagnosis.正在经历或面临发展迟缓或残障风险、需要支持以优化其发展、功能能力与生活质量的人；不限于有诊断者。许多儿童的支持需求与行为有关——儿童通过行为沟通，行为可能是对环境因素的回应。
Project budget
项目预算		All costs related to the child care project — cost estimates, professional fees, contingency, applicable taxes, capital expenses, fixtures, furnishings and equipment.涵盖托儿项目的全部成本，如造价估算、专业费用、应急费、适用税费、资本支出、固定装置、家具与设备。
Project lead
项目负责人		Responsible for all aspects of the project and its alignment with the project owner's vision, goals and objectives; may be an authorized signatory.负责项目的一切方面，确保项目与项目业主确定的愿景、目标与宗旨一致；可由业主或其代表担任，可为授权签字人。
Project owner
项目业主		The entity that determines the vision, goals and objectives for the project and has the authority to make decisions on the construction of the centre.决定项目愿景、目标与宗旨、并有权就托儿中心建设作决策的实体。
Project team
项目团队		The people who deliver the project deliverables on behalf of the project owner — direct employees or contractors such as a project manager or child care consultant.代表项目业主交付项目成果的人，可以是业主的直接雇员，或从头到尾支持项目的承包商，如项目经理或托儿顾问。
Schematic design
方案设计		A schematic design includes a complete description of building systems (structural, mechanical, HVAC, plumbing and electrical), interior and exterior finishes and the building site.包含建筑系统（结构、机械、暖通、给排水与电气）、室内外饰面与建筑场地的完整描述的设计。
Site plan
场地平面图		A graphic representation of all existing and proposed improvements to a site, drawn to scale, including landscaping, access pathways, terrain, parking and utilities, and illustrating zoning requirements.按比例绘制的、展示场地现有与拟建改进（建筑、室外游乐区、景观、出入路径与人行道、地形、所需停车与市政接驳）的图形，并说明分区要求。
Softscape
软质景观		The live horticultural elements of a landscape, such as grass/sod, plants, shrubs and trees.景观中的活体园艺元素，如草/草皮、植物、灌木与树木。
Space allocation
面积分配		Amount of area in square metres that should be provided for the specified space, excluding non-functional elements such as a long corridor within the space.应为指定空间提供的面积（平方米）；不含空间内为到达主要功能要素而可能需要的长走廊等非功能元素。
Universal design
通用设计		The design of products and environments to be usable by all people, to the greatest extent possible, without the need for adaptation or specialized design.让产品与环境在最大可能程度上可供所有人使用、无需适应性或专门设计的设计。见 Universal Design Network of Canada。
